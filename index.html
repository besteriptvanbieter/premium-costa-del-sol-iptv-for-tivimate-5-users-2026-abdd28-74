How to Stop ISP Throttling on This IPTV Service

Your internet provider may slow down streaming traffic. This can cause buffering. SubTV IP recommends two settings to avoid that:

  • Use Cloudflare DNS – set your device’s DNS to 1.1.1.1. This hides your streaming traffic from your ISP.
  • Set buffer / cache to 5,000 ms – in TiviMate, go to Settings > Playlists > your playlist > Buffer Size. Choose 5,000 ms (5 seconds). This gives the app time to load data before playing.
